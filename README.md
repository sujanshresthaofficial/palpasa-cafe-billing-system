# ☕ Palpasa Café — Billing System

> A modern digital billing system designed to simplify customer activity tracking, charge calculation, and invoice generation for businesses.

**Palpasa Café — Billing System** is a web-based billing system currently under active development. The project aims to replace repetitive manual billing processes with a streamlined digital workflow for managing customer transactions, calculating charges, and generating receipts or invoices.

> **Status:** 🚧 In Development — Early Stage

---

## 📌 Overview

Managing customer transactions manually can become time-consuming and difficult to maintain as business activity grows.

Palpasa Café — Billing System is being developed to provide a centralized digital solution that can help businesses:

* Track customer transactions and activity
* Calculate charges accurately
* Generate receipts and invoices digitally
* Reduce repetitive manual work
* Organize billing information efficiently
* Provide a foundation for future business analytics

The system is currently in its initial development phase, with the interface and core concepts being built before introducing additional technologies and backend functionality.

---

## ✨ Planned Features

The following features are part of the project's development roadmap:

* 🧾 **Digital Billing** — Create and manage customer bills digitally
* 💰 **Automatic Charge Calculation** — Calculate totals based on entered items and charges
* 👤 **Customer Management** — Maintain relevant customer information
* 📊 **Transaction Tracking** — Keep track of customer transactions and billing activity
* 🧮 **Invoice Generation** — Generate clear and structured invoices or receipts
* 📈 **Business Insights** — Analyze transaction and customer activity
* 🔍 **Search & Filtering** — Easily find customers and previous transactions
* 💾 **Data Management** — Store and manage billing records
* 📱 **Responsive Interface** — Provide a usable experience across different screen sizes

> Some of these features are planned and are **not yet implemented**.
